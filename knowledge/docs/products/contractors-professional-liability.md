# Contractors Professional Liability
*Synthetic product sheet.* Who it's for: general and specialty contractors that design, design-build, specify or manage construction (NAICS 2362xx, 2371xx, 2381xx), revenue $1M to $100M. Market in California: **non-admitted (surplus lines)**. Illustrative carriers: Harborline Specialty Insurance Co., Meridian Surplus Underwriters (fictional).

Covers claims arising from errors in design, specifications and construction management the contractor performs in-house or subcontracts. Fills the gap the GL professional services exclusion (CG 22 79) leaves.

Disclosures triggered in California: the D-1 surplus lines disclosure (Cal. Ins. Code §1764.1), signed at application unless the client is an industrial insured; the broker fee disclosure if the agency charges a fee (Cal. Ins. Code §1623(a)(3)).
