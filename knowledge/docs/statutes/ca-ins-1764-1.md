# Cal. Ins. Code §1764.1: surplus lines disclosure (excerpt)
*Public statutory text. Source: FindLaw copy of the California Insurance Code, current as of 2026-01-01. Verify against leginfo.legislature.ca.gov.*

**(a)(1), in part:** "... at the time of accepting an application for an insurance policy, other than a renewal of that policy, issued by a nonadmitted insurer, the signature of the applicant on the disclosure statement set forth in subdivision (b) is obtained."

**(a)(2):** "In a case in which the applicant has not received and completed the signed disclosure form required by this section, the applicant may cancel the insurance so placed. The cancellation shall be on a pro rata basis as to premium, and the applicant shall be entitled to the return of any broker's fees charged for the placement."

**(b):** the notice itself; see `knowledge/disclosures/ca-d1.md` for the verbatim text.

**(c)(1):** "An industrial insured is an insured that does both of the following: (A) Employs at least 25 employees on average during the prior 12 months. (B) Has aggregate annual premiums for insurance for all risks other than workers' compensation and health coverage totaling no less than twenty-five thousand dollars ($25,000) or obtains insurance through the services of a full-time employee acting as an insurance manager or a continuously retained insurance consultant." For an industrial insured, the notice goes on the confirmation, certificate of placement or policy, and no signature is required.

Product requiring it: any product placed with a nonadmitted insurer for a California home-state insured.
