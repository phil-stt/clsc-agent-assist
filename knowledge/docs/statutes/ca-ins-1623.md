# Cal. Ins. Code §1623: broker status and broker fees (excerpt)
*Public statutory text. Source: FindLaw copy of the California Insurance Code, current as of 2026-01-01. Verify against leginfo.legislature.ca.gov.*

**(a), in part:** "It shall be presumed that the person is acting as an insurance broker if the person is licensed to act as an insurance broker, maintains the bond required by this chapter, and discloses, in a written agreement signed by the consumer, all of the following: ... (3) The amount of all broker fees being charged by the person."

Demo reading: when the agency charges a broker fee on a placement, the fee goes in a written agreement the client signs, and the fact that a fee may be charged is disclosed with the initial quote. Regulations at 10 CCR §2189 et seq. add detail.
