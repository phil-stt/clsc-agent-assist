# Cal. Ins. Code §384: certificates of insurance (excerpt)
*Public statutory text. Source: FindLaw copy of the California Insurance Code, current as of 2026-01-01. Verify against leginfo.legislature.ca.gov.*

**(a):** a certificate of insurance shall contain the statement, or words to the effect: "This certificate or verification of insurance is not an insurance policy and does not amend, extend or alter the coverage afforded by the policies listed herein. Notwithstanding any requirement, term, or condition of any contract or other document with respect to which this certificate or verification of insurance may be issued or may pertain, the insurance afforded by the policies described herein is subject to all the terms, exclusions and conditions of the policies."

Summary of the wider rule: most states' certificate-of-insurance laws prohibit a certificate that misrepresents coverage; California's §384 sets the required disclaimer. The agency's E&O standard follows from both: a certificate may show only what the attached forms support, and issuing one is a licensed person's act.
