# CG 24 04: Waiver of Transfer of Rights of Recovery Against Others to Us
*Synthetic summary for the demo, not ISO wording.*

The insurer waives any right of recovery it may have against the person or organization shown in the Schedule because of payments made for injury or damage arising out of the named insured's operations or work done under a contract with that party.

Satisfies: waiver of subrogation, for the scheduled party.
