# CG 20 33: Additional Insured, Owners, Lessees or Contractors, Automatic Status When Required in Construction Agreement With You
*Synthetic summary for the demo, not ISO wording.*

Makes any person or organization an additional insured automatically when the named insured has agreed in a written construction agreement to add them, without naming them in a schedule. Status applies only while the named insured's ongoing operations for that party are being performed. It ends when the work is completed and does not extend to the products-completed operations hazard.

Satisfies: additional insured, ongoing operations, when a written construction agreement requires it. Does not satisfy: completed operations.
