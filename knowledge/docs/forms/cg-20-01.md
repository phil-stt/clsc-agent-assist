# CG 20 01: Primary and Noncontributory, Other Insurance Condition
*Synthetic summary for the demo, not ISO wording.*

Amends the Other Insurance condition so the policy is primary to, and will not seek contribution from, other insurance available to an additional insured, when the named insured has agreed in a written contract that this insurance would be primary and noncontributory.

Satisfies: primary and noncontributory, when a written contract requires it.
