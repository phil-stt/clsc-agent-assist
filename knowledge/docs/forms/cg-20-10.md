# CG 20 10: Additional Insured, Owners, Lessees or Contractors, Scheduled Person or Organization
*Synthetic summary for the demo, not ISO wording.*

Adds the person or organization named in the Schedule as an additional insured, but only for liability caused by the named insured's ongoing operations performed for that party. Coverage for the additional insured ends when the work is completed. It does not provide completed-operations additional insured status; that requires CG 20 37 or an equivalent.

Satisfies: additional insured, ongoing operations, for the scheduled party.
