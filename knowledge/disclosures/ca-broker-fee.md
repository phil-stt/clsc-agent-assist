Pacific Crest Insurance Services may charge a broker fee for placing this coverage. The fee is separate from the premium and is not refundable except as required by law. Before we charge any fee, we will give you a written broker fee agreement that states the amount, describes the services we perform for you as your broker, and discloses any compensation we may receive from the insurer. You will sign that agreement before the fee is charged. If you cancel a surplus lines policy under your five-day right, any broker fee will be returned to you.
